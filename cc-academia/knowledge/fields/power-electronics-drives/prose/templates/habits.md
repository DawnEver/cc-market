# Field habits: prose

- Numbers in abstract and conclusion: 24/46 abstracts and 26/46 conclusions give percent or absolute numbers, some
  with operating points; IEEE Trans. control papers expect them. Related: numbers.abstract-conclusion,
  position.detail-follows-position.
- Self-reference: IEEE Trans. house style is "this article"; the field mixes "paper", "work", "study" and "we".
  Related: self-reference.
- Enumerated lists: contribution lists (17/46) and numbered conclusion findings (14/46) are standard in IEEE Trans.
  Related: argument.no-enumerative-scaffolding, introduction.roadmap.
- Abstract opening: 12/46 abstracts open on the method ("This paper presents ..."). Related: structure.funnel-opening.
- Textbook material: about 30/46 papers re-derive VSD matrices, double Fourier integrals or MTPA equations.
  Related: audience.general-practitioner.
- Coined terms: paper-specific acronyms for single concepts are common. Related: terms.established-precise.
- Novelty: "novel" or "main contribution and novelty" appear in about 20/46. Related: argument.no-announced-novelty.
- Relative clauses and contrasts: ", which ..." tails and "while" contrasts occur in almost every paper; "whereas"
  in about 10/46. Related: sentence.relative-clause, sentence.no-contrast-join.
- Subjects: "the machine suffers from", "the strategy seeks", "enables" are routine. Related: wording.technical-verbs.
- Wording: "of paramount importance", "imperative", "remarkable" occur. Related: wording.plain.
