# Field habits: prose

- Roadmaps and step lists: a full roadmap paragraph in 25/35 papers; "first / then / finally" step lists in abstracts; section openers "In this section, ...". Related: introduction.roadmap, structure.no-structure-preview, argument.no-enumerative-scaffolding.
- Abstract opening: often on the method limitation rather than the broadest context; IEEE abstracts carry numbers. Related: numbers.abstract-conclusion, structure.funnel-opening.
- Self-reference: "this article", "this work" and "this contribution" are as common as "this paper". Related: self-reference.
- Novelty: announced ("The contribution of this article is", "novel" in 18/35, "originality", "first application"). Related: argument.no-announced-novelty.
- Method agents as verb subjects: "the optimiser finds", "the method ensures", "the algorithm returns" are routine. Related: wording.technical-verbs.
- Relative clauses: ", which" tails and "that" relatives are pervasive (about 18 "which" per paper). Related: sentence.relative-clause.
- Contrast: "However" in 32/35 papers, "whereas" in 10/35, "while" joins contrasts throughout. Related: sentence.no-contrast-join.
