# Conventions: prose

- Reporting verbs: propose, present, develop, introduce, derive, demonstrate, validate, compare; results "show / indicate / reveal" (related: wording.technical-verbs).
- Tense: present for method and figures; past or present perfect in conclusions (related: conclusion.tense).
- Person: "we" in all mathematics/IGA papers and about 60 % of IEEE TO papers; passive ("is employed", "is used") for set-up (related: self-reference).
- Hedging: light ("can", "may"); strength comes from comparisons, not adverbs (related: claim.hedging).
- Gap statements: mostly a limitation of a named method family; explicit "not studied" claims are rare (3/35) (related: argument.prior-work-as-found).
- Comparison with prior methods: "the proposed method" versus "a geometric template-based optimisation" or "classical FEM", with fairness conditions stated (same DOF, same torque, single thread) (related: argument.no-announced-novelty).
- Acronyms defined in the abstract and again at first use: SIMP, MMA, GCMMA, IGA, NURBS, DOF, TO.
