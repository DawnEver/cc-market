# Conventions: data

- Numbers: journal abstracts carry 1-3 percentages (often to two decimals); digests often give
  one headline percent; operating points and absolute values appear mainly in results (related: numbers.abstract-conclusion).
- Units in text with a space; speeds in r/min, rpm and krpm all occur (related: mech.unit-spacing, terms.one-canonical).
