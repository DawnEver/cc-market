# Section and paragraph shape

Source: common.

- A section starts with its first substantive fact, linked to what the reader knows (structure.no-structure-preview),
  never an umbrella sentence (structure.no-umbrella-opener), and closes with one causal sentence
  (paragraph.context-content-conclusion).
- A paragraph runs context, content, conclusion (paragraph.context-content-conclusion); each topic in one place
  (structure.one-place).
- A sentence opens on old information (cohesion.old-to-new), keeps subject and verb together
  (sentence.subject-verb-adjacent), puts the action in the verb (sentence.concrete-actor-action) and ends on the new
  fact (cohesion.old-to-new).
- Rewriting: skeleton of the reasoning first, then sentences; re-read the paragraph after any split or deletion
  (revision order: skills/write/revise.md).
- Results subsection headings follow {heading.style} (heading.style).
